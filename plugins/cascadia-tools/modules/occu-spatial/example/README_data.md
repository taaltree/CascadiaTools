# Example data: fisher camera-trap survey (SIMULATED)

**These data are simulated.** No real fisher survey is behind them. The study area, the
stations and the detections were all generated by `dev/occu-spatial/make_example.R`
(`set.seed(20251)`) so that the true parameter values are known and can be compared with the
estimates. `dev/occu-spatial/si_truth.R` replays that script and confirms that it reproduces
these files byte for byte. It writes the true values to `results/occu-spatial/si/truth.json`.

## The story

The fisher (*Pekania pennanti*) is a forest carnivore of conservation concern in the Pacific
Northwest. A field crew put out 160 baited camera stations across a 30 x 30 km block of the
western Cascades in summer 2024 (UTM zone 10N). Stations were at least 1 km apart. Each camera
ran for up to six weeks, and each week is one survey occasion. Some cameras failed early
(batteries, bears), and some weeks were only partly covered.

The question is: **is fisher occupancy associated with canopy cover and elevation, once we
allow for missed detections and for fishers being clustered in space beyond what those two
covariates explain?** A second aim is a map of predicted occupancy across the block.

## Design

| dimension | value |
|---|---|
| camera stations (sites) | 160 |
| weekly occasions per station | up to 6 (138 stations have 6; 22 have 2-5 because the camera failed) |
| station-weeks surveyed | 903 of 960 (5.9% missing) |
| study area | 30 x 30 km; station spacing 1.0-40.3 km (5% quantile 4.2 km) |
| occupancy covariates | canopy cover (%), elevation (m) |
| detection covariate | days the camera was working in that week (2-7) |
| prediction grid | 900 cells of 1 km^2 |

Raw summaries: the fisher was photographed at 70 stations (naive occupancy 0.44), with 140
detection-weeks in total.

## Files and columns

`camera_stations.csv`: one row per station (160 rows).

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `station` | station id, CAM-001 ... CAM-160 |
| `utm_e`, `utm_n` | UTM zone 10N easting and northing (m) |
| `canopy_cover_pct` | canopy cover around the station (%), mean 59.7, SD 12.9 |
| `elevation_m` | elevation (m), mean 1090, SD 246 |
| `ownership` | federal / state / private industrial (not used in the model; it has no effect in the simulation) |

`fisher_detections.csv`: one row per station-week that the camera was working (903 rows).

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `station` | station id (matches `camera_stations.csv`) |
| `week` | occasion 1-6 |
| `start_date` | first day of that week |
| `days_active` | days the camera worked that week (2-7; 91% of weeks are 7) |
| `detected` | 1 = fisher photographed at least once that week, 0 = not |

`prediction_grid_1km.csv`: 900 cells with `utm_e`, `utm_n` (cell centre, m), `canopy_cover_pct`
and `elevation_m`, drawn from the same habitat surfaces as the stations.

The same folder also holds the 12-species mesocarnivore data (`mesocarnivore_detections.csv`,
`config_multispecies.yaml`) and the hermit warbler integrated data (`warbler_*.csv`,
`config_integrated.yaml`). They are not part of this worked example.

## How the data were generated

- Canopy cover and elevation are smooth habitat surfaces. Canopy has a correlation range of
  6 km, and elevation has a 15-km range plus a rise to the north. The two are weakly
  correlated (r = -0.22 at stations).
- Occupancy: logit(psi) = -0.30 + 0.90 x canopy(SD units) - 0.40 x elevation(SD units) + w(s),
  where w is a residual spatial effect. It is a Gaussian process with exponential covariance,
  variance 1.0 and an effective range of 8 km.
- Each station is occupied (z = 1) with probability psi. Weekly detection is
  logit(p) = -1.0 + 0.45 x (days_active - 6.5) / 1.2, which gives p = 0.31 in a full 7-day week.
  The detection is y ~ Bernoulli(z x p).
- Camera failure: each station had a 15% chance that its camera failed at week 3-6 and recorded
  nothing after that. In 10% of weeks, the camera worked only 2-6 days.

## TRUE values (simulated), on the scale the module reports

The module standardises each covariate (mean 0, SD 1 over the stations, or over the surveyed
weeks for `days_active`). The true values are therefore expressed per 1 SD.

| estimand | truth | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `occupancy_prob` | **0.426** | Pr(occupied) at mean canopy (59.7%) and mean elevation (1090 m), spatial effect 0 |
| `psi_beta_canopy_cover_pct` | **0.90** | logit change per 1 SD (12.9%) of canopy cover; odds ratio 2.46 |
| `psi_beta_elevation_m` | **-0.40** | logit change per 1 SD (246 m) of elevation; odds ratio 0.67 |
| `detection_prob` | **0.285** | weekly detection at the mean days_active (6.71 days) |
| `p_beta_days_active` | **0.38** | logit detection change per 1 SD (1.01 days) of camera days |
| `spatial_variance` | **1.0** | variance of the residual spatial effect (logit scale) |
| `effective_range_km` | **8** | distance at which residual spatial correlation falls to 0.05 |
| `mean_occupancy` | **0.483** | mean of the true psi over the 160 stations (includes w) |
| `prop_sites_occupied` | **0.5125** | 82 of 160 stations were truly occupied (realised) |

Only 70 of the 82 occupied stations produced a photo. The other 12 are occupied stations where
the fisher was missed, which is why naive occupancy (0.44) is below the truth (0.51).
